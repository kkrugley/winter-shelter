# Family Shelter (6 mm Plywood)

Large outdoor shelter for an entire colony (3–5 cats). Designed for laser cutting from 6 mm birch plywood. Features separate compartments, a second-floor level, and easy-access cleaning.

| Spec | Value |
|------|-------|
| Capacity | 3–5 cats |
| Dimensions | 80 × 50 × 45 cm |
| Material | 6 mm birch plywood (recommended) |
| Assembly time | ~2 hours |
| Build cost | ~€40 |

## Contents

- **CAD source files** — `.m3d`, `.a3d`, `.cdw` (Designed in Kompas 3D)
- **DXF parts** — 40 numbered part files in `DXF1.0/`
- **Nested layouts** — ready-to-cut files for multiple sheet sizes in `DXF1.0/NEST/`
- **Nesting model** — `DXF1.0/Family-Shelter-Nest.3dm` (Rhino 3D)
- **Warning plate designs** — vector files in `PlateDesign/` (DXF, EPS, PDF, SVG)
- **Engraving artwork** — `Engraving_design-eng.svg` (English)

## Assembly

### Tools & Materials Needed

- PVA wood glue (D3 class)
- Rubber mallet
- Sandpaper (P80–P120)
- Weather-resistant, animal-safe wood sealant (optional)
- Textile-based insulation: crumpled paper, cotton fabric, synthetic winteriser (dry and clean)
- Strong glue or sealant for securing bowls
- Stainless steel or ceramic bowls

### Step-by-Step Assembly

See the full illustrated manual at `website/public/files/SafePawsManual.pdf` (pages 10–17, Russian + English). The Family shelter has 18 assembly steps:

1. **Assemble leg supports** — connect parts 01.01 to 01.01-01 (similar to Cozy but sized for the larger base).
2. **Attach base** — install part 00.01 on the supports.
3. **Install interior walls** — fit parts 00.05, 00.06, 00.06-01, and 00.07.
4. **Install second-floor supports** — fit parts 05.02 and 05.02-01.
5. **Install second floor** — fit part 05.01 (measure 12 mm for support clearance).
6. **Install roof on the room wall.**
7. **Install outer wall panel** — fit part 01.11-01.
8. **Assemble the arch** — join parts 04.01 and 04.02.
9. **Attach wall supports** — install three identical 00.11 parts.
10. **Install wall 00.06-02.**
11. **Install exterior walls** — fit parts 00.02, 00.03, and 00.04.
12. **Secure bowl holder assemblies** — install assemblies 03.00.
13. **Install roof** — fit part 00.08.
14. **Place warning plate** — attach to the roof.
15. **Secure bowls** — place bowls in holders and secure with strong glue or sealant.

**Insulation:** Fill all wall cavities with dry textile-based insulation. The larger surface area makes insulation even more critical for thermal performance.

## Installation Tips

- Choose a quiet, hidden spot away from roads and dogs — the larger size requires more careful placement.
- Orient the entrance away from prevailing wind.
- Raise the shelter above ground (bricks, wooden blocks).
- The larger interior means more cats can share body heat — place bedding in both compartments.
- Check periodically, refresh bedding, and repair any damage.
