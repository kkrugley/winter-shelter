# Cozy Shelter (6 mm Plywood)

Compact outdoor shelter for 1–2 cats. Designed for laser cutting from 6 mm birch plywood (recommended thickness for durability).

| Spec | Value |
|------|-------|
| Capacity | 1–2 cats |
| Dimensions | 40 × 40 × 30 cm |
| Material | 6 mm birch plywood |
| Assembly time | ~2 hours |
| Build cost | ~€25 |

## Contents

- **CAD source files** — `.m3d`, `.a3d`, `.cdw` (Designed in Kompas 3D)
- **DXF parts** — 21 numbered part files in `DXF1.0/`
- **Nested layouts** — ready-to-cut files for multiple sheet sizes (750×750, 1300×900, 1500×750, 1525×1525 mm) in `DXF1.0/NEST/`
- **Nesting model** — `DXF1.0/nest.3dm` (Rhino 3D)
- **Warning plate designs** — vector files in `PlateDesign/` (DXF, EPS, PDF, SVG)
- **3D model** — `CozyShelter_v1.stp`

## Assembly

### Tools & Materials Needed

- PVA wood glue (D3 class)
- Rubber mallet
- Sandpaper (P80–P120)
- Weather-resistant, animal-safe wood sealant (optional)
- Textile-based insulation: crumpled paper, cotton fabric, synthetic winteriser (dry and clean)
- Stainless steel or ceramic bowls (Ø up to 12 cm, rim wider than 12 cm)

### Step-by-Step Assembly

See the full illustrated manual in the `DXF1.0/Instructions-SafePaws/` directory of the 3 mm variant or the project root `website/public/files/SafePawsManual.pdf` (17 pages, Russian + English). The assembly sequence is identical for both thickness variants:

1. **Assemble leg supports** — connect parts 01.01 to 01.01-01 for 4 corner legs + 1 central cross support.
2. **Attach base** — install part 00.01 on the supports.
3. **Install interior walls** — fit parts 00.05, 00.06, 00.07, and 00.06-01.
4. **Assemble the arch** — join parts 04.01 and 04.02.
5. **Install the arch** — position on the base.
6. **Attach wall 00.06-02.**
7. **Install exterior walls** — fit parts 00.02, 00.03, and 00.04.
8. **Place inner roof** — install part 00.09.
9. **Assemble bowl holder** — connect parts 03.01 and 03.02.
10. **Install bowl holder assembly** — position assembly 03.00.
11. **Install roof** — fit part 00.08.
12. **Place warning plate** — attach to the roof.

**Insulation:** Fill the 40 mm gap between double walls with dry textile-based insulation (fluffy, not compressed).

## Installation Tips

- Choose a quiet, hidden spot away from roads and dogs.
- Orient the entrance away from prevailing wind.
- Raise the shelter above ground to avoid standing water.
- Check periodically, refresh bedding, and repair any damage.
